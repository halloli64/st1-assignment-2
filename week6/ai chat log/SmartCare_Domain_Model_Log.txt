SmartCare Community Clinic Domain Model Discussion Log

User Request:
I am modelling a simple domain model for the SmartCare community clinic.
Using ONLY the confirmed requirements FR-01 to FR-10 below, suggest:
1. The main domain classes needed.
2. The main responsibilities of each class.
3. The relationships between the classes and their multiplicities.
For every class and relationship you suggest, give the relevant requirement ID(s) that support your suggestion.
Keep the model simple and suitable for the first version of a small community clinic. Do not invent requirements or add unnecessary manager, controller or engine classes.

Requirements:
FR-01: Manage patient records.
FR-02: Search for patients.
FR-03: Manage practitioner profiles.
FR-04: Manage practitioner availability.
FR-05: Create appointments using a patient, practitioner and available time.
FR-06: Prevent double booking.
FR-07: Reschedule/edit appointments while maintaining history.
FR-08: Cancel appointments.
FR-09: Retain appointments for viewing and reporting.
FR-10: View practitioner schedules and appointment status.

Copilot Response:

MAIN DOMAIN CLASSES

Class: Patient
Responsibilities: Store and maintain patient record details; support patient lookup/search.
Requirements: FR-01, FR-02

Class: Practitioner
Responsibilities: Store practitioner profile details; maintain practitioner availability; support viewing schedules.
Requirements: FR-03, FR-04, FR-10

Class: AvailabilitySlot
Responsibilities: Represent a practitioner's available date/time period that can be used for booking appointments.
Requirements: FR-04, FR-05

Class: Appointment
Responsibilities: Record bookings between patients and practitioners; store appointment date/time and status; support rescheduling, cancellation, history retention, and reporting.
Requirements: FR-05, FR-06, FR-07, FR-08, FR-09, FR-10

Class: AppointmentHistory
Responsibilities: Keep a record of appointment changes (e.g., reschedule/edit actions) to preserve history over time.
Requirements: FR-07

RELATIONSHIPS AND MULTIPLICITIES

Relationship: Patient - Appointment
Multiplicity: Patient (1) to Appointment (0..*)
Reason: A patient may have many appointments; each appointment is for exactly one patient.
Requirements: FR-05, FR-09

Relationship: Practitioner - Appointment
Multiplicity: Practitioner (1) to Appointment (0..*)
Reason: A practitioner may have many appointments; each appointment is with exactly one practitioner.
Requirements: FR-05, FR-10

Relationship: Practitioner - AvailabilitySlot
Multiplicity: Practitioner (1) to AvailabilitySlot (0..*)
Reason: A practitioner can define multiple available time slots. Each slot belongs to one practitioner.
Requirements: FR-04

Relationship: Appointment - AvailabilitySlot
Multiplicity: Appointment (1) to AvailabilitySlot (1)
Reason: An appointment is created using an available time slot. This also helps support prevention of double booking by ensuring a slot is allocated to at most one appointment.
Requirements: FR-05, FR-06

Relationship: Appointment - AppointmentHistory
Multiplicity: Appointment (1) to AppointmentHistory (0..*)
Reason: An appointment can have multiple historical change records; each history record belongs to one appointment.
Requirements: FR-07

Relationship: Patient - Practitioner
Multiplicity: Many-to-many (via Appointment)
Reason: Patients and practitioners become associated through appointments rather than a direct relationship.
Requirements: FR-05
